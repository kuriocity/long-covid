# 02 — Treatments by Evidence Tier

Everything with a plausible claim in male AGA, sorted by how much the evidence actually supports it. Categories from [01-mechanism.md](01-mechanism.md): **Halt** (stops progression), **Regrow** (recovers miniaturized follicles), **Enabler** (makes another drug work better), **Adjunct** (small independent effect), **Restore** (surgical).

---

## Tier 1 — Proven, multiple RCTs, regulatory approval

| Intervention | Category | Effect size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Finasteride 1 mg oral** | Halt (+ modest Regrow) | Halts progression in ~90%; visible regrowth in ~2/3 at vertex over 1–2 yrs | FDA-approved for AGA. Serum DHT ↓ ~71%. The reference standard. |
| **Minoxidil 5% topical** | Regrow | Modest but real density gain; best in vertex | FDA-approved, OTC. BID dosing. Needs ~4 months before any read. |
| **Dutasteride 0.5 mg oral** | Halt (+ Regrow) | **Superior to finasteride** on hair count in RCTs; 2025 network meta-analysis ranks it the most effective monotherapy | Off-label for AGA in most markets (approved for AGA in South Korea/Japan). Serum DHT ↓ ~90–98%. |
| **Hair transplant (FUE/DHI)** | Restore | Definitive for bald scalp; graft survival 90%+ at good clinics | Only intervention that puts hair where none remains. Requires lifelong medical therapy alongside, or the *native* hair around the grafts keeps receding. |

**Combination finasteride + minoxidil beats either alone** — this is consistently shown and is the standard of care, not an aggressive protocol. A 2025 SR/MA also supports minoxidil–finasteride *mixed topical solution* over minoxidil solution alone.

## Tier 2 — Good evidence, RCT-supported, usually off-label or adjunctive

| Intervention | Category | Evidence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low-dose oral minoxidil (LDOM) 1.25–5 mg** | Regrow | Multiple cohorts + single-arm meta-analysis; 2024–2025 expert consensus statement on dosing. Large RCTs still lacking. | **Once-daily oral replaces twice-daily topical.** Adverse effects: hypertrichosis ~24% (dose-dependent, face/body), transient shedding 16–22%, mild peripheral edema ~2%. Contraindicated in pericardial disease, uncontrolled hypertension. Serious cardiac events rare at alopecia doses. |
| **Microneedling + minoxidil** | Enabler | 2025 SR/MA: 12 RCTs, 631 patients, SMD **1.32** (95% CI 0.73–1.92) for hair count vs minoxidil alone. Caveat: heterogeneity I² = 88% — the direction is solid, the magnitude isn't. | Benefit seen at all tested depths and durations. 1.0–1.5 mm, weekly-to-fortnightly. Cheap, self-administered. |
| **Topical finasteride 0.25%** | Halt | Phase III RCT: efficacy comparable to oral; plasma Cmax >100× lower; serum DHT ↓ 34.5% vs 55.6% oral; sexual AEs 2.8% vs 4.8% | The dose-sparing option. **2025 FDA flagged unvalidated pharmacy-compounded formulations** — use a trial-validated product if available. |
| **PRP (activated)** | Adjunct | 2025 SR/MA: 43 RCTs, 1877 patients. Activated PRP increases hair density at 3 and 6 months; non-activated PRP had more adverse effects and less benefit. | Real but modest and **temporary** — requires maintenance sessions. Typical protocol: 3–4 sessions at 4-week intervals, then every 4–6 months. Highly variable preparation protocols across clinics = highly variable results. |
| **Ketoconazole 2% shampoo** | Adjunct | Systematic review (2020) + 2025 review: increased hair shaft diameter and pilary index; adjunct benefit alongside finasteride | Near-zero cost, near-zero risk. 2–3×/week, 3–5 min contact time. Use it — the only argument against is that the effect is small. |

## Tier 3 — Weak, mixed, or mechanism-only evidence

| Intervention | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **LLLT / laser caps (650–680 nm)** | Several small RCTs positive, FDA-cleared devices exist, but effect sizes are small and industry-funded trials dominate. Reasonable if you already own a device; poor value as a primary purchase. |
| **Topical melatonin 0.0033–0.1%** | Head-to-head: **significantly inferior to minoxidil 5%** on 24-week hair count. Viable only as an alternative for minoxidil-intolerant patients. |
| **Topical caffeine** | Mechanism-plausible (phosphodiesterase inhibition), clinical data limited and mixed. Fine as a shampoo ingredient, not a treatment. |
| **Rosemary oil** | One frequently-cited trial vs minoxidil 2%; network meta-analyses place it below conventional agents. Not a finasteride/minoxidil substitute. Harmless. |
| **Saw palmetto** | Weak 5-AR inhibition, low-quality trials. Notably: it **shares the neurosteroid mechanism** of finasteride, so it is *not* a "natural, side-effect-free" alternative — see [07-5ari-neuro-overlap.md](07-5ari-neuro-overlap.md). |
| **Biotin** | Only works if you are biotin-deficient. You almost certainly aren't. Also interferes with thyroid and troponin lab assays — stop 3 days before bloodwork. |
| **Pyrithione zinc** | Anti-dandruff, marginal AGA data. |
| **Exosome / stem-cell "therapies"** | No approved product, no adequate RCTs, priced like a proven treatment. Not recommended at 2026 evidence levels. |
| **Dermarolling without a drug** | Pointless for AGA. Microneedling's evidence is as a *penetration enhancer and wound-healing stimulus alongside minoxidil*, not as monotherapy. |

## Tier 4 — Fix if broken, otherwise irrelevant

These don't treat AGA. They cause a *separate, additive* hair loss (telogen effluvium) that makes AGA look worse and can mask treatment response. Worth testing once:

| Marker | Why |
|---|---|
| **Ferritin** | Low iron stores → telogen effluvium. Target >50–70 ng/mL for hair, higher than the lab's "normal." |
| **TSH, free T4** | Both hypo- and hyperthyroidism shed hair |
| **Vitamin D** | Receptor is required for follicle cycling; deficiency common |
| **Zinc, B12** | Deficiency-driven shedding |
| **Total/free testosterone** | Baseline before any 5-ARI change; also relevant to the LC picture |

Note: the long COVID project already calls for TSH, ferritin, free T, vitamin D, B12 and cortisol. **One blood draw covers both projects** — see `personal/action-plan.md`.

## What to actually do — the decision tree

```
Are you willing to take a systemic 5-ARI?
├── YES → Dutasteride 0.5 mg (most effective) or Finasteride 1 mg (better-characterised safety)
│         + Minoxidil (oral LDOM if adherence is the problem, topical 5% otherwise)
│         + Ketoconazole 2% shampoo 2-3x/week
│         + Microneedling 1.0-1.5 mm weekly-to-fortnightly
│         + Tretinoin 0.025% before minoxidil IF minoxidil response is poor
│         → reassess with standardised photos at 6 and 12 months
│         → crown still cosmetically insufficient at 12-18 months? → PRP, then consider transplant
│
├── NO, TOPICAL ONLY → Topical finasteride 0.25% + minoxidil + ketoconazole + microneedling
│                      Accept: slower, weaker, less certain. Still far better than minoxidil alone.
│
└── NO ANTI-ANDROGEN AT ALL → Minoxidil (topical or LDOM) + microneedling + ketoconazole + PRP
                              Accept: you are slowing, not halting. Progression continues.
                              → plan for transplant earlier, and expect to need more than one
```

**The two rules that matter more than which drug you pick:**

1. **A 5-ARI you take 70% of the time beats a perfect protocol you abandon.** Adherence dominates drug selection in real-world outcomes. Choose the regimen you will actually execute on a bad week.
2. **Never judge a change before 6 months, and never on a mirror.** Standardised photos, same lighting, same angles, same length, dry hair. Everything else is mood.

## Timeline expectations

| Month | What you should see |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | Nothing. Possible **dread shed** on starting minoxidil or a 5-ARI — synchronised telogen release. It's a sign the drug is working on the cycle. Do not stop. |
| 2–4 | Shedding settles. Possibly less hair in the drain. |
| 4–6 | First honest read on photos: texture, some vellus→terminal conversion |
| 6–12 | Peak visible density gain from drugs |
| 12–18 | Plateau. What you have now is roughly your medical ceiling. |
| 18+ | Maintenance. Any further gain needs a procedure, not a stronger drug. |
