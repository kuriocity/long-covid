## 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors — efficacy

- [The efficacy and safety of dutasteride compared with finasteride in treating men with androgenetic alopecia: a systematic review and meta-analysis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.2147/CIA.S192435) — *Clinical Interventions in Aging*, 2019. **Systematic review / meta-analysis.** Dutasteride more efficacious than finasteride; similar adverse-reaction rates including sexual dysfunction. ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30863034/))
- [Dutasteride May Be Most Effective Monotherapy for Androgenetic Alopecia in Men](https://www.dermatologyadvisor.com/news/dutasteride-most-effective-monotherapy-androgenetic-alopecia-men/) — Dermatology Advisor, reporting a **network meta-analysis**, 2025. Dutasteride 0.5 mg/day ranked most effective monotherapy.
- [Efficacy and safety of twice- or thrice-weekly dutasteride versus daily finasteride in men with androgenetic alopecia: a randomized, investigator-blinded, active-controlled, parallel-group pilot study](https://www.jaadinternational.org/article/S2666-3287(25)00101-4/fulltext) — *JAAD International*, 2025. **Pilot RCT.** Dose-dependent effect across dutasteride arms; thrice-weekly dutasteride did not significantly exceed daily finasteride 1 mg on hair count. ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12547282/))
- [Finasteride and Dutasteride for the Treatment of Male Androgenetic Alopecia: A Review of Efficacy and Reproductive Adverse Effects](https://gmr.scholasticahq.com/article/88531-finasteride-and-dutasteride-for-the-treatment-of-male-androgenetic-alopecia-a-review-of-efficacy-and-reproductive-adverse-effects) — *Georgetown Medical Review*. **Narrative review.**
- [Comparing minoxidil-finasteride mixed solution with minoxidil solution alone for male androgenetic alopecia: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12537375/) — 2025. **Systematic review / meta-analysis.** Combination topical superior to minoxidil alone.
- [Updates in Treatment for Androgenetic Alopecia](https://anndermatol.org/DOIx.php?id=10.5021%2Fad.25.042) — *Annals of Dermatology*, 2025. **Review.**
- [Androgenetic Alopecia: An Update on Pathogenesis and Pharmacological Treatment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12380480/) — 2025. **Review.** Mechanism + pharmacology.

## 5-ARI pharmacokinetics and dosing

- [AVODART (dutasteride) FDA Prescribing Information](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2008/021319s015lbl.pdf) — FDA label. **Regulatory document.** Terminal half-life ~5 weeks at steady state; single-dose half-life 3–4 days; serum DHT suppression data.
- [DHT rebound minimal after missed 5-ARI doses](https://www.urologytimes.com/view/dht-rebound-minimal-after-missed-5-ari-doses) — *Urology Times*. **PK analysis reporting.** Weekly dutasteride 3.5 mg produces DHT close to daily 0.5 mg.
- [Dutasteride for Hair Loss: Dosage, Efficacy & Side-Effects](https://ishrs.org/patients/treatments-for-hair-loss/medications/dutasteride/) — **ISHRS** (International Society of Hair Restoration Surgery). **Professional society patient information.** Notes common off-label twice-weekly dosing due to long half-life.
- [Finasteride Versus Dutasteride for Hair Loss: 2026 Clinical Decision Framework with Intermittent Dosing and Potency Data](https://charlesmedicalgroup.com/plastic-surgery/finasteride-versus-dutasteride-hair-loss-comparison/) — Charles Medical Group, 2026. **Clinic review — commercial source, cross-check claims.** Useful summary of intermittent-dosing rationale.
- [Dutasteride Half-Life: How It Influences Hair Loss Treatment](https://wimpole.com/blog/dutasteride-half-life/) — Wimpole Clinic. **Clinic explainer — secondary source.**

## Topical finasteride

- [Efficacy and safety of topical finasteride spray solution for male androgenetic alopecia: a phase III, randomized, controlled clinical trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34634163/) — 2021. **Phase III RCT.** Efficacy comparable to oral; plasma Cmax >100× lower; serum DHT −34.5% vs −55.6% oral; sexual AEs 2.8% vs 4.8%. ([full text PDF](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/355181979_Efficacy_and_safety_of_topical_finasteride_spray_solution_for_male_androgenetic_alopecia_a_phase_III_randomized_controlled_clinical_trial))
- [Topical finasteride for male and female pattern hair loss: Is it a safe and effective alternative?](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35238144/) — 2022. **Review.**
- [Comparative Efficacy of Topical Finasteride (0.25%) in Androgenetic Alopecia](https://journals.lww.com/ijot/fulltext/2023/15020/comparative_efficacy_of_topical_finasteride.3.aspx) — *International Journal of Trichology*, 2023. **Comparative trial.**
- [FDA Concerns Over Topical Finasteride](https://www.baumanmedical.com/fda-warning-topical-finasteride-holistic-treatment-options/) — Bauman Medical, 2025. **Clinic reporting of a 2025 FDA communication** on unvalidated pharmacy-compounded topical finasteride, citing sexual and mood disorder concerns.

## 5-ARI neuropsychiatric effects

- [5α-Reductase Isoenzymes: From Neurosteroid Biosynthesis to Neuropsychiatric Outcomes](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12921826/) — **Mechanism review.** Type 1 vs type 2 isoenzyme distribution; allopregnanolone synthesis; GABA-A modulation.
- [Depression risk of 5-alpha reductase inhibitors: A systematic review and meta-analysis with a focus on comparator groups](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.08.21.25333991v1.full) — *medRxiv*, 2025. **Systematic review / meta-analysis — PREPRINT, not peer-reviewed.** Addresses comparator-group confounding.
- [Signal Detection of Depression and Suicidality Associated with Finasteride and Dutasteride: Updated Pharmacovigilance Evidence and Recommendations for Comprehensive Psychiatric Assessment](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3425/16/4/394) — *Brain Sciences*, 2026. **Pharmacovigilance / disproportionality analysis.** Signals for both drugs; recommends psychiatric assessment.
- [Post-finasteride syndrome: pharmacists must lead the conversation on patient safety](https://pharmaceutical-journal.com/article/opinion/post-finasteride-syndrome-pharmacists-must-lead-the-conversation-on-patient-safety) — *The Pharmaceutical Journal*. **Opinion/review.** Documents the **2025 EMA confirmation** of a finasteride–suicidal ideation association and FDA/MHRA label-warning strengthening; PFS symptom list.
- [The connection of 5-alpha reductase inhibitors to the development of depression](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0753332221008842) — *Biomedicine & Pharmacotherapy*. **Review.**

## 5-ARI depression — comparative studies

- [Suicidal risk associated with finasteride versus dutasteride among men treated for benign prostatic hyperplasia: nationwide cohort study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-32356-3) — *Scientific Reports*, 2023. **Nationwide cohort study** (France), men ≥50 initiating finasteride 5 mg or dutasteride 0.5 mg 2012–2016; outcome suicide death or hospitalisation for self-harm. Increased risk with **finasteride relative to dutasteride** among men with prior mood disorders; authors note this is equally consistent with finasteride harm or dutasteride protection. ⚠️ BPH population, finasteride at 5 mg (5× the AGA dose), observational. ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10066399/))
- [Depression risk associated with the use of 5α-reductase inhibitors versus α-blockers: A retrospective cohort study in South Korea](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8926175/) — **Retrospective cohort.** No difference in depression risk vs alpha-blockers after propensity-score matching.

## Minoxidil — oral and topical

- [Oral minoxidil for hair loss: Expert consensus statement](https://www.epocrates.com/online/article/oral-minoxidil-for-hair-loss-expert-consensus-statement) — **Expert consensus.** Off-label LDOM 0.625–5 mg/day range for adults/adolescents.
- [Efficacy and safety of low-dose oral minoxidil in the management of androgenetic alopecia](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38315101/) — *Expert Opinion on Pharmacotherapy*, 2024. **Review.** Dosing escalation 1.25 → 2.5 → 5 mg; favourable efficacy/safety profile.
- [Efficacy and safety of oral minoxidil in the treatment of alopecia: a single-arm rate meta-analysis and systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12188453/) — 2025. **Single-arm meta-analysis.** Hypertrichosis ~24%, shedding 16–22%, peripheral edema ~2%; pericardial effusion rare at alopecia doses.
- [Oral Minoxidil for Alopecia Treatment: Risks, Benefits, and Recommendations](https://vivo.weill.cornell.edu/display/pubid41118052) — Weill Cornell. **Review.** Notes large RCTs and evidence-based guidelines are still lacking.
- [Expanding the therapeutic landscape of minoxidil for androgenetic alopecia: topical, oral and sublingual formulations](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12898826/) — **Review.** Sulfotransferase activation mechanism.
- [Low Dose Oral Minoxidil in Androgenic Alopecia (NCT07018349)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07018349) — **Ongoing trial registration.**

## Tretinoin + minoxidil

- [Tretinoin enhances minoxidil response in androgenetic alopecia patients by upregulating follicular sulfotransferase enzymes](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/dth.12915) — Sharma et al., *Dermatologic Therapy*, 2019. **Clinical study.** SULT1A1 activity ↑1.8× within 5 days; 43% of predicted non-responders converted to responders.
- [Efficacy of Topical Tretinoin and Topical Minoxidil Cotherapy in Androgenetic Alopecia: A Review](https://karger.com/sad/article/doi/10.1159/000553018/951022/Efficacy-of-Topical-Tretinoin-and-Topical) — *Skin Appendage Disorders* (Karger). **Review.** Combination clinical data minimal; does not support first-line dual therapy; reasonable as adjunct in minoxidil non-responders; ~3× increase in systemic minoxidil absorption when tretinoin applied first. ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42535115/))
- [The efficacy of tretinoin gel 0.1% followed by topical minoxidil-5% in individuals with androgenetic alopecia](https://www.ijord.com/index.php/ijord/article/view/2012) — *Int J Res Dermatol*. **Small clinical study.**

## Microneedling

- [Evaluating the efficacy and safety of combined microneedling therapy versus topical minoxidil in androgenetic alopecia: a systematic review and meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40056230/) — *Archives of Dermatological Research*, March 2025. **Systematic review / meta-analysis.** 12 RCTs, 631 patients; SMD 1.32 (95% CI 0.73–1.92) for hair count vs minoxidil alone; I² = 88%; benefit at all depths and durations.
- [Same study, BJD conference abstract (P06)](https://academic.oup.com/bjd/article-abstract/194/1/ljaf429.034/8415335) — *British Journal of Dermatology*, 2026.

## PRP

- [Platelet-Rich Plasma in the Management of Alopecia: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Clinical Evidence](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13555-025-01542-8) — *Dermatology and Therapy*, 2025. **Systematic review / meta-analysis.** 43 RCTs, 1,877 participants; activated PRP effective for density and recurrence; non-activated PRP more adverse effects.
- [Platelet-Rich Plasma for Androgenetic Alopecia: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37533146/) — 2023. **Systematic review / meta-analysis.** Density increase at 3 and 6 months vs placebo.
- [Meta-Analysis of Efficacy of Platelet-Rich Plasma Combined with Minoxidil for Androgenetic Alopecia](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38789807/) — 2024. **Meta-analysis.**
- [Efficacy of Platelet-Rich Plasma as an Adjunct to Hair Transplantation: A Systematic Review](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12506585/) — 2025. **Systematic review.**

## Ketoconazole and OTC agents

- [Role of Topical Ketoconazole in Therapeutic Hair Care Beyond Seborrhoeic Dermatitis and Dandruff](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jvc2.70026) — Gupta, *JEADV Clinical Practice*, 2025. **Review.** Androgen-receptor inhibition and anti-inflammatory rationale in AGA.
- [Topical ketoconazole for the treatment of androgenetic alopecia: A systematic review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/dth.13202) — Fields et al., *Dermatologic Therapy*, 2020. **Systematic review.** Increased hair shaft diameter and pilary index.
- [Ketoconazole as an adjunct to finasteride in the treatment of androgenetic alopecia in men](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0306987703002640) — *Medical Hypotheses*. **Hypothesis paper.**
- [Comparative Effect of Conventional and Non-Conventional Over-the-Counter Treatments for Male Androgenetic Alopecia: A Systematic Review and Network Meta-Analysis](https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/26/16/7920) — *IJMS*, 2025. **Network meta-analysis.** Minoxidil 5% significantly superior to topical melatonin on 24-week hair count; positions rosemary oil and other alternatives below conventional agents.
- [Hair Loss Shampoos: Clinical Evidence Review 2026](https://www.myhairline.ai/blog/hair-loss-shampoos-evidence) — **Secondary review — commercial source.** Notes limited/mixed data for caffeine, biotin, saw palmetto, pyrithione zinc.

## Frontal / temporal region specifics

- [Dutasteride for the Treatment of Androgenetic Alopecia: An Updated Review](https://karger.com/drm/article/240/5-6/833/913258/Dutasteride-for-the-Treatment-of-Androgenetic) — *Dermatology* (Karger). **Review.**
- [Superiority of dutasteride over finasteride in hair regrowth and reversal of miniaturization in men with androgenetic alopecia: A randomized controlled open-label, evaluator-blinded study](https://ijdvl.com/superiority-of-dutasteride-over-finasteride-in-hair-regrowth-and-reversal-of-miniaturization-in-men-with-androgenetic-alopecia-a-randomized-controlled-open-label-evaluator-blinded-study/) — *IJDVL*. **Open-label RCT, evaluator-blinded.**

- [Mature Hairline vs Receding Hairline: How To Tell the Difference](https://www.happyhead.com/blog/mature-hairline) and [Temporal recession vs true hairline recession: how to diagnose](https://www.myhairline.ai/blog/what-is-temporal-recession-vs-true-hairline-recession-diagnosis) — **Commercial/clinic explainers — secondary sources.** Used only for the maturation-vs-AGA clinical distinction (1–2 cm regression then stabilisation; progression + leading-edge miniaturization as the discriminator), which is standard dermatological teaching. Replace with a primary reference when one is identified.
- ⚠️ The frequently-cited figure that 5% minoxidil improved frontal-scalp hair density in **58% of men at 48 weeks** (attributed to *JAAD*) circulates via [clinic secondary sources](https://ro.co/hair-loss/does-minoxidil-work-on-hairline/). **Primary paper not verified for this document — treat as indicative only.** Same for the Cochrane observation that the crown evidence base substantially exceeds the frontal one.

## Source-quality key

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Systematic review / meta-analysis** | Strongest here |
| **RCT / Phase II–III** | Strong, check n and endpoint |
| **Regulatory document** | Authoritative for PK and label facts |
| **Expert consensus** | Useful where RCTs are absent (e.g. LDOM dosing) |
| **Review / narrative** | Context, not evidence |
| **Preprint** | Not peer-reviewed — flagged inline |
| **Pharmacovigilance** | Signal detection only; cannot establish causation or incidence |
| **Clinic / commercial** | Treat as marketing; used here only for pricing and pipeline dates, flagged inline |
