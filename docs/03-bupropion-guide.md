# Bupropion (Wellbutrin) for Long COVID — Practical Reference

## When bupropion may be indicated for long COVID

The **dopamine-deficit residual phenotype** — anhedonia + amotivation + hypersomnia + emotional flatness + no PEM — maps to dopamine + norepinephrine deficit in prefrontal cortex + striatum (see [`01-triad-model.md`](01-triad-model.md) for mechanism). This is exactly what bupropion targets.

Uniquely, bupropion:
- Is a **norepinephrine-dopamine reuptake inhibitor (NDRI)** — direct dopamine effect
- Does NOT cause emotional blunting (unlike SSRIs — which can worsen anhedonia)
- Reduces cytokines → bonus for residual neuroinflammation
- Is stimulating/activating → helps hypersomnia
- Is well-tolerated long-term

Post-COVID central hypersomnia case series showed favorable response to bupropion and methylphenidate. Bupropion is NOT indicated for the PEM-dominant / ME-CFS-like LC phenotype, where activating drugs can trigger crashes.

**This is a general reference, not a personalized prescription. Always coordinate with a physician.**

## Pre-start checklist (must clear before Rx)

### Hard contraindications — do NOT take if any of these apply:
- History of seizures (any type)
- Eating disorder history (anorexia, bulimia)
- Currently detoxing from alcohol / benzos / barbiturates
- Recent MAOI use (< 14 days)
- Severe head injury, brain tumor, AVM, severe stroke
- Uncontrolled hypertension
- Bipolar disorder (without mood stabilizer)
- Heavy alcohol drinker

### Before starting:
- [ ] Full medical history disclosed to doctor
- [ ] Baseline blood pressure recorded
- [ ] Full med + supplement list shown to pharmacist
- [ ] Alcohol plan agreed (reduce/stop or steady, no binges)
- [ ] Someone knows I'm starting a new medication
- [ ] Follow-up scheduled at 4 weeks and 8 weeks

## Formulation choice: XL (extended release)

| Type | Dosing | My use? |
|---|---|---|
| IR | 3x/day | ❌ Higher seizure risk |
| SR | 2x/day | Backup option |
| **XL** | **1x/day AM** | ✅ **My choice — smoothest levels, lowest seizure risk** |

**Ask for:** Wellbutrin XL 150 mg (generic OK)

---

## Dosing protocol

- **Week 1:** 150 mg XL every morning
- **Week 2 onwards:** if tolerated + needed → 300 mg XL every morning
- **Absolute max:** 450 mg/day (seizure risk jumps 10x above 450)
- **Go slower if sensitive:** 150 mg every OTHER day for week 1, then daily

### Critical timing rules
- **Take AFTER breakfast** (not fasted)
- **Morning only** — never after noon (destroys sleep)
- If XL causes insomnia → switch to SR AM + no-later-than-2pm split
- **Swallow whole** — crushing/chewing = full dose at once = seizure risk

## Common side effects (first 1-4 weeks, usually settle)

| Side effect | Management |
|---|---|
| Dry mouth | Hydrate, sugar-free gum, xylitol lozenges |
| Insomnia | Take earlier in AM; dose reduction if persists |
| Anxiety/jitteriness | Usually settles 1-2 weeks; slow titration if bad |
| Headache | Hydration; usually resolves |
| Nausea | Take with food |
| Dizziness | Hydration; monitor BP |
| Constipation | Fiber, water, magnesium |
| **Reduced appetite** | See appetite management section below |
| Increased sweating | Usually mild |

**If side effects don't settle in 2-3 weeks or worsen → call doctor.**

---

## SERIOUS side effects — ER / doctor immediately

- **Seizure** — twitching, LOC, unusual muscle activity
- **Hypertensive spike** — severe headache + vision changes + chest pain
- **Suicidal thoughts** or worsening depression
- **Manic symptoms** — decreased sleep + euphoria + racing thoughts + risky behavior
- **Allergic reaction** — rash, swelling, breathing difficulty
- **Sudden severe agitation or hallucinations**

---

## Seizure risk factors — AVOID or minimize

- **Heavy alcohol** — especially binges + stopping
- **Abrupt benzo/sleep-med withdrawal** (Xanax, Valium, Ambien)
- **Tramadol** — huge seizure risk combined
- **High-dose stimulants** (careful combos with Ritalin/Adderall)
- **Recreational drugs** — cocaine, MDMA, amphetamines
- **Diet pills, high-caffeine energy drinks**
- **Excessive caffeine** — keep <400 mg/day
- **Fasting + intense exercise** — can lower seizure threshold
- **Sleep deprivation**

**Practical rule:** Social drinking OK at 1-2 max, never binge, never stop cold if daily drinker.

---

## Drug interactions

### Dangerous — avoid
- MAOIs (14-day washout)
- Linezolid, methylene blue
- Thioridazine (contraindicated)

### Needs dose adjustment
**CYP2D6 substrates (bupropion strongly inhibits CYP2D6):**
- Metoprolol, propranolol, carvedilol
- Codeine, tramadol (also seizure risk), oxycodone
- Many antidepressants (SSRIs, tricyclics)
- Tamoxifen (bupropion may reduce efficacy)

**CYP2B6 modifiers:**
- Inducers (lower bupropion): ritonavir, efavirenz, carbamazepine, rifampin
- Inhibitors (raise bupropion): clopidogrel, ticlopidine

**Show full med list to pharmacist before starting.**

| Supplement | Bupropion combo? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **L-Tyrosine** | ✅ Safe | No documented interaction, theoretically synergistic (both boost dopamine) |

| **Glycine** | ✅ Safe | Night dose helps offset bupropion insomnia |
| **CoQ10, NR/NMN, D-ribose, B vitamins** | ✅ Safe | All fine |
| **LDN** | ✅ Safe | Commonly combined in LC clinics |
| **5-HTP, St. John's Wort** | ⚠️ Discuss | Theoretical serotonin issues |
| **High-dose caffeine / pre-workouts** | ⚠️ Moderate | Additive stimulation and BP |
| **Nicotine (patches, gum)** | ⚠️ Bupropion is anti-nicotine at receptors |

## Appetite loss management

~30% of people get appetite suppression on bupropion. **For the long COVID dopamine-deficit phenotype, undereating is counter-productive** because:
- Dopamine synthesis needs tyrosine from protein
- Undereating triggers cortisol → worsens HPA
- Low blood sugar → more brain fog
- Muscle loss → worse mitochondrial capacity
- Poor sleep

### Rules:
1. **Eat by clock, not hunger.** Alarms at 8 AM, 12 PM, 4 PM, 7 PM
2. **Take bupropion AFTER breakfast** — never fasted
3. **Front-load calories in morning** — biggest meal within 90 min of waking
4. **Liquid calories** when solid food hard:
   ```
   Best "not hungry" smoothie:
   - 1 banana
   - 1 cup oats OR ½ cup Greek yogurt
   - 1 scoop whey/plant protein (25-30g)
   - 2 tbsp nut butter
   - 1 cup whole milk / oat milk
   - 1 tbsp honey or dates
   - Handful of berries
   → ~600-700 cal, 35g protein
   ```
5. **Calorie-dense foods:** nuts, nut butters, seeds, avocado, olive oil, whole eggs, cheese, dark chocolate, granola, dried fruit, hummus
6. **Protein target: 1.6-2.0 g/kg body weight daily**
   - 70kg → 110-140g protein/day
   - Spread across 4 meals ~30g each
   - Tyrosine-rich sources: eggs, chicken, turkey, cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, fish, tofu, cheese, pumpkin seeds
7. **Track weight weekly.**
   - Losing >2 lb/week for 3+ weeks → tell doctor
   - Losing >5% body weight in 3 months → dose reduction
8. **If severe:** drop to 150 mg XL, or switch to SR split, or add mirtazapine at night (appetite + sleep)
9. **RED FLAG:** if you start liking being not hungry / restricting on purpose → STOP + tell doctor (eating disorder history is a hard contraindication)

## Blood pressure monitoring

- Baseline before starting
- Weekly for 4 weeks
- Monthly after
- **Flag:** systolic >140 or diastolic >90 consistently
- Resting HR >100 consistently

## Timeline of what to expect

| Timeframe | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Days 1-3 | Mild activation, possible headache, dry mouth |
| Week 1 | Sleep changes possible, appetite may drop |
| Week 2-3 | Side effects usually settle. Some early energy lift. |
| **Week 4-6** | **Meaningful mood + motivation + anhedonia improvement typically starts** |
| Week 8-12 | Full effect. If not clearly helping at 8 weeks/300mg → discuss changes |

**Don't judge in first 2 weeks.** Often feels rough early (anxious, jittery, insomnia) then gets much better.

---

## Stopping bupropion

- Minimal withdrawal vs SSRIs — no long taper needed
- Don't stop cold turkey without doctor
- Standard: 300 → 150 for 1-2 weeks → stop
- **If anhedonia/fatigue returns → I needed it, can restart**

---

## Escalation if bupropion alone insufficient after 8 weeks at 300 mg

Discuss with doctor in order:
1. **Bump to 450 mg XL** (max, careful monitoring)
2. **Add modafinil 100-200 mg AM** — hypersomnia component. Great combo.
3. **Add low-dose methylphenidate** — for residual amotivation
4. **Add LDN 1.5-4.5 mg** — microglial/neuroinflammation component
5. **Reassess labs** — untreated thyroid/low T/low ferritin limits response
6. **Consider stellate ganglion block** — direct vagal/autonomic intervention

## Lifestyle amplifiers (crucial for maximum bupropion effect)

- **Hydrate 2.5-3 L/day** — dry mouth worse if dehydrated
- **Protein-heavy breakfast** with dose → tyrosine substrate for dopamine
- **Zone 2 cardio + resistance** amplified when combined with bupropion
- **Reduce alcohol** — 2-3 drinks noticeably worse on bupropion
- **Tighten sleep hygiene** — cool room, no screens 90 min before bed, magnesium/glycine
- **Morning sunlight + cold shower** — synergistic with bupropion's dopamine effect
