### Antihistamines and mast-cell drugs in long COVID
- [Wall EC et al. — STIMULATE-ICP: famotidine+loratadine, colchicine, rivaroxaban vs no drug in long COVID](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42419335/) — *Lancet Infect Dis* 2026. **Open-label phase 3 RCT, n=778.** Fam-lor FAS −1.48 (−2.88 to −0.08) at 12 wk; no difference at 24 wk.
- [Salvucci F et al. — Antihistamines improve cardiovascular manifestations and other symptoms of long COVID](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10388239/) — *Front Cardiovasc Med* 2023. Retrospective, 14 vs 13. Fexofenadine 180 + famotidine 40 × 20 d.
- [Glynne P et al. — Long COVID following mild SARS-CoV-2 infection: characteristic T cell alterations and response to antihistamines](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34611034/) — *J Investig Med* 2022. Observational, 72% improved.
- [Weinstock LB et al. — Mast cell activation symptoms are prevalent in long COVID](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34563706/) — *Int J Infect Dis* 2021. Online survey, n=136 LC.
- [Lee E, Picard M — MCAS: a practical review using ECNM-AIM criteria](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12639879/) — *Allergy Asthma Clin Immunol* 2025. Counter-view; ketotifen/cromolyn dosing.
- [Augustin M et al. — Intestinal mast-cell activation, tryptase, zonulin and viral protein in post-COVID syndrome](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41794369/) — *Mucosal Immunol* 2026. **Ileal biopsies 15–22 months post-infection.**
- [Lenning OB et al. — Serum tryptase and CPA3 not different in long COVID](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39285602/) — *Scand J Immunol* 2024. Negative, n=24 vs 24.
- [Tsilioni I, Theoharides TC — SARS-CoV-2 spike stimulates human mast cell release of IL-1β, chymase, tryptase](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37298438/) — *Int J Mol Sci* 2023. In vitro.
- [Czarnetzki BM — Ketotifen in urticaria pigmentosa](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6341102/) — *Dermatologica* 1983. Crossover, n=10.
- [Schwarzer G et al. — Ketotifen for asthma in children (Cochrane)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14973969/) — sedation 21% vs 12%, weight gain 27% vs 17%.
- [Soter NA et al. — Oral disodium cromoglycate in systemic mastocytosis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/111124/) — *NEJM* 1979, n=5. [Horan RF et al. 1990](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2110198/) — n=11, GI symptoms p<0.02.
- [Christoforou ME et al. — High-dose oral cromolyn in ME/CFS with MCAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12924640/) — *Cureus* 2026, case series n=5.
- [Siebenhaar F et al. — Rupatadine in mastocytosis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23734572/) — *Allergy* 2013, placebo crossover n=30; GI not improved.
- [FDA — Boxed warning for montelukast neuropsychiatric events](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-communications/fda-requires-boxed-warning-about-serious-mental-health-side-effects-asthma-and-allergy-drug) — 2020.
- [Weng Z et al. — Quercetin is more effective than cromolyn in blocking human mast cell release](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3314669/) — *PLoS One* 2012. In vitro + 2 pilots; ~1% oral absorption; industry-supplied product.
- [Johnston CS et al. — Vitamin C 2 g/day lowers blood histamine 38%](https://doi.org/10.1080/07315724.1992.12098241) — *J Am Coll Nutr* 1992, n=10.
- [Reese I et al. — Guideline on management of suspected adverse reactions to ingested histamine (DGAKI/GPA/AeDA/SGAI)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34651098/) — *Allergol Select* 2021. 3-phase diet; DAO supplements not recommended; serum DAO no diagnostic value.
- [Sánchez-Pérez S et al. — Low-histamine diets: is the exclusion of foods justified by their histamine content?](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33919293/) — *Nutrients* 2021. Only 32% of excluded foods justified.
- [Komericki P et al. — DAO supplementation, placebo-controlled crossover](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21165702/) — 2011, n=39.
- [Clark JH et al. — H2-receptor antagonist tachyphylaxis: scoping review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/lary.70337) — *Laryngoscope* 2026. ~20% loss by day 15 (acid endpoints).
- [Nwokolo CU et al. — Intermittent vs continuous H2RA dosing preserves response](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1889719/) — *Gastroenterology* 1991.
- [SIGHI — Histamine elimination diet leaflet](https://www.histaminintoleranz.ch/downloads/SIGHI-Leaflet_HistamineEliminationDiet.pdf) — patient organisation; freshness rules.
