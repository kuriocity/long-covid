## 2026-09-08 (evening) — 📚 Research sweep: mast cell / gut / dopamine axis / India availability → plan written

Six parallel literature and market sweeps (mast-cell treatments, gut repair, dopaminergic/autonomic drugs, India drugs, India supplements/probiotics, India labs/devices/SGB). Output: `docs/05-mast-cell-gut-protocol.md` + phased plan at the top of `personal/action-plan.md`.

### Corrections to what was written earlier today
- **Expired fish oil is not a histamine trigger.** Refined oil has no histidine; histamine cannot form in it. Scombroid histamine forms in fish *flesh*. What forms in old oil is lipid peroxides/aldehydes — an irritant. The entry below over-claimed. The observation still counts — it fits **mast-cell / vagal-afferent reactivity** (irritant-triggered), which is also what pineapple (liberator), cocoa and tea (little histamine) point to. Net reframe: **"histamine intolerance / DAO deficiency" → "hyper-reactive gut mucosal mast cells signalling via sensitised vagal afferents."** DAO supplements are irrelevant; stabilisation + barrier repair + trigger removal are the levers; H1/H2 blockade helps only insofar as histamine is a mediator at the afferent (it is one of them — H1 antagonists block the ~1-minute afferent response in animal work)
- **The Tier 1 antihistamine evidence was overstated in `docs/02`.** STIMULATE-ICP (published July 2026, n=778, open-label): famotidine + loratadine gave −1.5 fatigue points vs no drug, below the ~4-point meaningful change, and no difference at 24 weeks. Unselected patients; the food-reactive subgroup has never been trialled. Case series (57–79% fog/fatigue improvement) are tiny and retrospective. **This is an n-of-1 experiment with a fast readout, not an evidence-based treatment**

### What the sweep changed
- **taVNS downgraded** — COVIVA 2026 null; three other controlled trials null
- **LDN**: no RCT has reported (Vancouver n=160 finished Feb 2026, unpublished); the "52%" was an open pilot. Still ranked first for Phase 3 on safety and breadth
- **Gut**: one real RCT (SIM01 synbiotic, n=463, OR ~2 for fatigue/memory/concentration) — not obtainable in India; Darolac is the clean domestic approximation; VSL#3 (sold here) has a small positive LC RCT but histamine-flagged strains

## 2026-09-08 — 🧪 Histamine hypothesis: 3 triggers, plain-water control negative, first blocker dose taken

### Triggers to date (all: fog onset within MINUTES)
| Date | Trigger | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Aug 2026 | **Yakult** (L. casei, fermented) | Histamine-**containing** |
| Sep 2026 | **Chai / black tea** | Liberator + amines (confounded: caffeine, sugar, milk, tannins) |
| Sep 2026 | **Pineapple** | **Classic histamine LIBERATOR** |
| Sep 2026 | **Hot chocolate** | **Liberator + biogenic amines** (tyramine, phenylethylamine) |
| Sep 2026 | **EXPIRED omega-3 / fish oil** | ⭐ **Strongest data point.** Fish is among the highest-histamine foods and histamine forms *as fish degrades* (cf. scombroid poisoning). **"Expired" is a freshness control, not a food-list match** — same substance, different degradation state, different outcome. This is what the histamine mechanism predicts and what dairy/allergy explanations cannot account for. Also directly validates the fresh-vs-leftovers principle |

**Open question:** did **fresh** omega-3 ever cause fog? If tolerated fresh and only the expired bottle triggered, that is a **within-substance control** — everything held constant except histamine content — and would be the single best piece of evidence in this thread.

**Alternative hypothesis considered and rejected: dairy.** Three of four triggers contain milk (Yakult, chai, hot chocolate) — but **pineapple does not.** Dairy explains 3/4; histamine explains 4/4. Histamine is more parsimonious. ⚠️ If plain milk ever triggers fog, revisit as a possible second, independent intolerance.

**Temperature already excluded** by the negative plain-hot-water control, so hot chocolate being hot does not reopen it.

### Controls and reasoning
- **Plain hot water: NEGATIVE (no fog).** Rules out temperature, volume, gastric distension, vagal stretch reflex → it is the substance, not the act of drinking
- **Minutes-level onset** rules out reactive hypoglycemia (1–2 h) and fits poorly with postprandial hypotension (15–45 min, nutrient-driven)
- **Only property common to all three triggers is histamine.** Temperature, caffeine, sugar and volume each fail to explain all three
- **Reacting to liberators (pineapple) as well as histamine-containing foods → not pure DAO deficiency; implies a mast cell reactivity component.** Treatment consequence: stabilisation (ketotifen, cromolyn) may matter more than blockade alone; DAO enzyme supplements would NOT help liberator triggers

### Prior famotidine self-trial (historical, uncontrolled)
Initial subjective benefit, later Famocid 40 "didn't fix much." **Does NOT refute the hypothesis** — H2 monotherapy leaves H1 unblocked, and H2RA tachyphylaxis (receptor upregulation) is well documented. Uninterpretable, but an initial response nudges the prior up.

- **Fexofenadine 120 mg split in half (= 60 mg) + famotidine.** Reported feeling better; user's read is that it isn't placebo
- ⚠️ **Not the controlled test.** No trigger challenge, no written baseline. This is the setup where expectancy effects are strongest — noted without dismissing the report

- ⚠️ **Watch for famotidine tachyphylaxis** on continuous daily dosing — this already appears to have happened once
- ⚠️ **B12:** famotidine reduces gastric acid → impairs B12 absorption. B12 deficiency itself causes fatigue and fog. **Get the baseline B12 (already on the panel) before this becomes chronic**

### The test that would actually settle it
**Take the blockers, then deliberately eat Yakult or pineapple.** If the fog does not appear, that is placebo-resistant in a way "felt better" is not — the triggers produce a fast, specific, reproducible symptom.

### ⚠️ Sequencing consequence

Antihistamines improve fatigue and brain fog (STIMULATE-ICP RCT), which map onto energy / mental exhaustion / sleep in the weekly rating set — so the two cannot run concurrently. **This should be a decision, not a drift.**
